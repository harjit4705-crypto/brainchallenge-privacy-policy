Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Daily Brain Challenge. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We keep our data collection strictly to a minimum. When you sign in to our application via Google Sign-In, we only collect:

  • Email Address: Used solely to authenticate your identity, manage your user account, and save your app progress/scores locally and securely.

We do not collect your personal contacts, precise location, financial details, or any other sensitive device permissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

The email address collected is strictly used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate you and grant secure access to the app.
  • To track and persist your quiz challenges, scores, and progress.
  • To prevent fraudulent activities and secure user sessions.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, trade, or rent your email address or personal data to any third parties.

We use trusted third-party service providers solely to facilitate basic app functionalities:

  • Firebase / Google Sign-In: For secure user authentication and Google account integration.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Your authentication session is managed safely on your device using encrypted storage practices.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. If you wish to delete your stored email address or account details from our records, you can contact us directly. Upon receiving your request, we will delete your associated data promptly.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action to remove it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted here.
